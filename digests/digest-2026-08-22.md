  DeepMind recaps 15 years of games-as-testbed research, from the original Atari DQN agents through to partnerships with commercial game studios. The new push is prototyping AI-driven gameplay directly with studios, including the persistent multiplayer economy of EVE Online. Games remain useful because they give long-horizon, adversarial, partially observable environments with cheap resets and clear reward signal. **Why it matters:** Complex live games are the closest public proxy for long-horizon agent behaviour, so studio partnerships turn agent research into an evaluated product surface rather than a benchmark exercise.
