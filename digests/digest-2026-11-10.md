## 📄 Research Papers — Top Picks
- **PriorityFEC: Deadline-Aware Streaming FEC for KV-Cache Handoff in Edge Multi-Agent Systems** — Han Shen, Qifu Tyler Sun, Fanyang Meng et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21390
  When collaborative edge LLM agents pass task context to each other, the receiver normally repeats prefill; reusing the sender's KV cache avoids that but must arrive before decoding starts. Under strict deadlines and lossy edge links a full transfer may be impossible, so PriorityFEC prioritizes the most useful KV blocks and adds adaptive forward-error-correction loss recovery. **Why it matters:** Makes KV-cache sharing between agents viable on unreliable networks, cutting multi-agent handoff latency.
- **When Disclosure Is Compulsory: Evaluating Ride-Hailing Rebalancing Policies with Self-Interested LLM Driver Agents** — Xinyu Wang, Andres Fielbaum · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41132
  The EU Platform Work Directive will force ride-hailing platforms to disclose the main parameters of their automated decisions, which drivers could hand to an LLM to act on. The authors simulate drivers as self-interested LLM agents and compare a reactive versus an anticipatory rebalancing policy when the rules are public versus hidden. **Why it matters:** An early look at how transparent rules interact with LLM agents optimizing for individual gain.
- **STAR: Steering Action Representations with Proprioception for VLA Inference** — Guangyu Zhao, Yongchang Zhang, Haoxuan Ru et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41009
  Probing shows that late-layer action representations in vision-language-action models lose part of their task-relevant visual grounding. STAR is a training-free inference method that applies an exponential moving average over late-layer residual updates so action representations inherit object-aware directions from earlier layers. **Why it matters:** A cheap, no-retraining fix to improve pretrained robot VLA policies.
- **Radial Kernel Attention for Global Feature Exchange in Polymer Machine Learning Force Fields** — Wenbo Fu, Chenghui Mao, Haifeng Lv et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41675
  Polymer ML force fields usually rely on local neighbor graphs, while full all-pair attention is quadratic. Radial Kernel Attention maps interatomic distances from the cluster centroid to learned radial features and combines their kernelized overlap with content-dependent queries and keys, giving global feature exchange at linear cost in atom count. **Why it matters:** A linear-time global attention pattern that may transfer to other long-range scientific ML problems.
- **Interpretability-by-Design with Accurate Locally Additive Models and Conditional Feature Effects** — Vasilis Gkolemis, Loukas Kavouras, Dimitrios Kyriakopoulos et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16503
  GAMs are interpretable but underfit with feature interactions, while GA²Ms gain accuracy by adding pairwise terms at an interpretability cost. CALMs allow several univariate shape functions per feature, each active in a region defined by simple logical conditions, staying near GAM interpretability with near-GA²M accuracy. **Why it matters:** A middle ground for tabular models where explanations must stay human-readable.
