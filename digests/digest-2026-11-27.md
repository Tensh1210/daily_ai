## 📄 Research Papers — Top Picks
- **Replay, Don't Resample: Off-Policy Consolidation of Long-Horizon RL Experts** — Yue et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24059
  The paper proposes multi-expert replay policy distillation (MRPD), which merges per-domain RL experts into one model by replaying their training logs through the student in the original order, with no new sampling, environment calls or reward model. Consolidation costs about a seventh of the compute used to train the experts. On reasoning, search and SQL agentic domains it yields the best student, beating online RL baselines and on-policy multi-teacher distillation, and log order matters more than log amount or rewards. This is a v2 revision. **Why it matters:** Expert training logs could be reused as a curriculum to make model consolidation much cheaper.
- **KnowMe Paper Radar: Lightweight Discovery of Promising Papers in the Age of Autoresearch** — Chen et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23702
  KnowMe is an arXiv discovery tool that weights a paper's topical relevance by the reputation of its authors and institutions, and spreads author credit along collaborations to surface rising researchers. In a sealed test on 10,386 new papers, those with an identity-bound author were twice as likely to reach the top tenth of early citations. Its growing scholarly knowledge graph is meant to be queryable by research agents through a CLI. This is a v3 revision. **Why it matters:** Offers a day-one filter for the preprint flood, before citations exist, though it relies on author reputation as the quality signal.
- **Auditable Clinical Support with Decision Agents** — Bao et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23700
  Decision2Care is a decision agent that assigns a probability to every option at each step instead of only producing text. It diagnosed 546 emergency admissions across 12 diseases with 86.5% accuracy in a median of 2.2 s, versus 86.1–91.7% and more than 90 s for language-model agents. The authors also report guideline adherence and mortality-prediction results, and trace its four confident errors each to a single imaging report. This is a v3 revision. **Why it matters:** Much faster, step-by-step reviewable agent decisions, though prospective trials are still needed.
