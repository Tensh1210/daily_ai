## 📄 Research Papers — Top Picks
- **ReTerm: Recovery Training for Self-Improving Terminal Agents** — Wei Xie, Kaituo Feng et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18484
  Terminal agents often fail on long runs because they cannot recover from command errors or faulty implementations. ReTerm builds ReTerm-10K recovery demonstrations for SFT cold start, then uses ReTerm-GRPO, which forks from the agent's own failure states to sample independent recovery attempts. ReTerm-9B (Qwen3.5-9B) hits 34.8% Pass@1 on Terminal-Bench 2.0, +14.6 over base; code, data and model released. **Why it matters:** Error recovery is the main failure mode of coding agents, and this gives an open recipe to train it directly.
- **WorkRover: Scaling Long-Horizon Cowork Agent Training Via Recursive Environment Evolution** — Hang He, Linting Wang et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18067
  A framework where construction agents jointly build and validate work contexts, executable tasks and evidence-grounded verifiers, then iteratively repair them. Factorial Compatibility Replay picks the smallest validated repair across component revisions, and Verifier-to-Turn Credit Assignment adds per-turn signals from deterministic workspace-progress checks on top of GRPO. **Why it matters:** Environment quality is the bottleneck for long-horizon agent RL; self-repairing environments make scaling them tractable.
- **Tev: Scaling Long-Horizon Agentic Environments with Dense Progress Rewards** — Wenbo Hu, Shraman Pramanick et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18868
  Tev-Gym provides 1,170 verified long-horizon task environments across 7 domains, each rendered from a hidden "latent world" specification that yields a natural progress metric. Progress-potential reward shaping turns that metric into dense per-step rewards, making otherwise-intractable tasks trainable. Tev-27B shows large gains on Agents' Last Exam computing/math domains. **Why it matters:** Dense, verifiable rewards address the sparse-reward problem that blocks open models on hours-long agent tasks.
- **DeltaTTG: Training Multi-Turn Agents with a Token-to-Go Progress Meter** — Xuyan Ye, Xiyan Fu et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.17666
  Learns a reusable progress meter that predicts Token-to-Go (tokens remaining until task completion) from diverse policies' recorded trajectories. During RL, changes in the frozen meter's estimates redistribute outcome advantages across steps without changing the reward. Beats GRPO by 5.7/7.2 points on ALFWorld, PaperSearchQA and BFCLv3 with Qwen3-4B/Llama-3.1-8B at similar GPU cost. **Why it matters:** Cheap step-level credit assignment for multi-turn tool-calling agents without task-specific heuristics or external judges.
- **Open-o3-Video-v2: Seek, Ground, and Reason with Verifiable Spatio-Temporal Evidence** — Jiahao Meng, Zhongyuan Bao et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.17656
  Recasts video reasoning as an agentic process: the model detects missing information, uses tool calls to fetch more observations, and writes timestamped, box-level evidence into its reasoning trace. Clue-Seek GRPO rewards evidence coverage and penalizes redundant retrieval. The 4B model beats its Qwen3-VL-4B backbone by 8.5/12.6 points on V-STaR. **Why it matters:** Verifiable, actively gathered evidence makes video reasoning auditable rather than a black-box answer.
- **Learning How to Remember: Memory Modality Selection for GUI Agents** — Taeyun Roh, Suhyeong Park et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.17928
  A systematic study showing the best memory representation (text vs. visual) for VLM-based GUI agents depends on the interaction step. SwitchMem routes between textual and visual retrieved memory per step and outperforms static-memory baselines on three GUI benchmarks. **Why it matters:** Memory format is a per-step design decision for computer-use agents, not a fixed architecture choice.
- **JevGPT: Thinking Fast and Slow in Autonomous Research** — Yasheng Sun, Yanning Dai et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18272
  A dual-system research agent: System Two sets evidence objectives and integrates findings, while parallel System One workers locate support, judge relevance and guide further inspection. It leads on fixed-corpus QA and document ranking while finishing faster and cheaper than comparable research agents. **Why it matters:** Closing the gap between what retrieval finds and what the agent actually uses is key to cost-efficient deep-research agents.
- **Rubrics from the Task, Verdicts from Evidence: Pointwise Rubric Rewards with Tool-Augmented Judges** — Tianci Liu, Haoyu Wang · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18766
  ReVeR derives hard rules and graded principles from the instruction alone (avoiding response-induced bias) and scores each response independently with tool-augmented judging. Trained via label-blind distillation, it beats pairwise rubric RMs on preference accuracy and downstream policy optimization. It also finds that preference accuracy, rubric fidelity and downstream utility are distinct properties. **Why it matters:** Tool-using pointwise judges give RL a more reliable reward for instruction-following than pairwise preferences.
- **DoubtLess: Training-Free Contrastive Decoding for Efficient Reasoning** — Minghao Luo, Liang Chen · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18862
  A small reasoning model reads the large model's CoT twice — prompted brief and prompted verbose — and the logit difference steers the large model toward brevity at each step. It cuts Qwen3-32B CoT length by 31% with unchanged accuracy across seven benchmarks and reduces thinking tokens by 20% in a self-debugging coding agent. **Why it matters:** A drop-in inference-time way to cut reasoning cost without retraining the model.
- **VideoJev: System One Model for Video Understanding and Interaction** — Yuhan Zhu, Xiangyu Zeng et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18615
  Replaces autoregressive answer decoding in MLLMs with a shared head that scores temporal intervals and options against video evidence, unifying temporal grounding and multiple-choice video QA. Cached and joint inference reuse visual computation across queries. Gains of 6.7–10.8 mIoU on grounding and >100× speedups on video QA versus thinking-mode backbones. **Why it matters:** Gives visual agents fast, structured decisions over video history instead of slow free-text generation.
- **TacitVLA: Reasoning Vision-Language-Action Models Without Reasoning Latency** — Jack Lu, Yurong You et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.17540
  Shows reasoning supervision improves the VLA backbone even if actions never attend to the generated reasoning, so the action expert can act without waiting for decoding. Matches explicit-reasoning counterparts at up to 3.4× lower latency and sets new results on RoboMME Counting (82.7%) and RMBench (77.5%). **Why it matters:** Removes the latency tax that made reasoning VLAs impractical for real-time robots and driving.
- **AesCode: Aesthetic Code Generation with Decoupled Cross-Modal Rewards** — Xin Gao, Yifan Yang et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18860
  Generates slides, posters and dashboards as code using an image generated from the same prompt as aesthetic guidance. A design graph makes properties attributable; deterministic verifiers score parsable properties while a VLM judge with sample-specific rubrics scores the rest, optimized jointly via RL. **Why it matters:** Combines editability of code with visual quality of image models for document/visual artifact generation.
- **Fish Audio Drama 3 Technical Report** — Dawei Han, Shidong Li et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18010
  Open-source speech generation model on a 35B MoE (3B active) hybrid linear-attention backbone, supporting multi-reference voice cloning, natural-language direction, multi-turn revision, scene dubbing, infilling edits and streaming. Trained with pre-training, instruction fine-tuning and RL on a single learned human-preference reward; decodes 271 frames/s on one H200. **Why it matters:** A strong open-weight option for directed, editable TTS with fast inference via SGLang-Omni.
- **EgoExplorer: A Vision-Motion-Touch Observatory for Human Loco-Manipulation In Situ** — Yunsong Zhou, Xuekun Jiang et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18872
  A 100-hour wearable-captured dataset aligning egocentric video, whole-body motion, finger articulation and touch for professional activities, with step-level descriptions. A Calibrate-Complete-Constrain pipeline reduces body-point error by 71.9% vs. the strongest baseline. **Why it matters:** Richer multimodal human demonstrations are a key input for learning humanoid loco-manipulation.
