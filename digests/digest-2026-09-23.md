## 🚀 Product & Breakthroughs
- **Introducing GPT-6 Sol and Luna** — OpenAI · https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna
  OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna, two frontier models tuned for different capability/cost trade-offs for everyday work. Sol targets peak capability while Luna offers a cheaper, faster tier for high-volume tasks. **Why it matters:** A two-tier GPT-6 lineup lets teams pick cost vs. capability per workload instead of one-size-fits-all pricing.

  GPT-6 ships improved prompt caching with higher cache-hit rates, new diagnostics, and explicit cache breakpoints. This gives developers finer control to cut latency and cost on repeated-context workloads like agents and RAG. **Why it matters:** Explicit cache breakpoints + diagnostics make cost/latency tuning for long-context agent loops much more predictable.

## 📄 Research Papers — Top Picks
- **Qwen3.8-Omni: Towards Native Omni-Modal Agents** — Qwen Team · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25611
  Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash is a natively multimodal MoE agent model extending Qwen3.8-Next with a 1M-token context, built for long-horizon agentic tasks across text, audio, and video. It ships with Qwen-MM-Plugins and Qwen-Live-Harness, open-source frameworks for real-time multimodal agent orchestration (memory, tool use, sub-agent delegation). **Why it matters:** One of the first open frameworks treating real-time multimodal interaction as a full agent-orchestration problem, not just a bigger model.
- **Qwen-Audio-3.1-Realtime: Towards Reliable Agentic Voice Interaction** — Qwen Team · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25176
  Qwen-Audio-3.1-Realtime combines supervised fine-tuning, multi-teacher on-policy distillation, and GRPO-based tool-use training to build a voice assistant that reasons, acts, and speaks reliably. It raises task success from 78.4% to 82.0% and cuts erroneous interruptions on background speech from 73% to 13%. **Why it matters:** Sharp drop in false interruptions is a real usability fix for full-duplex voice agents, not just an accuracy bump.
- **Coding Agents are Strong Prompt Optimizers** — Singh et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26261
  The paper shows a coding agent can directly synthesize an optimized prompt from a static trajectory corpus, no environment access or iterative search needed. This Coding-Agent Skill Distillation (CASD) beats GEPA and SkillOpt on most of 4 agentic benchmarks at ~22x lower cost (~$1.60/pass). **Why it matters:** Cuts prompt-optimization cost by over an order of magnitude, making offline prompt tuning viable for teams without live eval infra.
- **Benchmark Radar: A Living Database and Search Engine for AI Benchmarks and Evaluation** — Wu et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11115
  Benchmark Radar is a searchable, continuously-updated catalog of AI benchmarks (LLM eval, agentic/tool-use, coding, safety) pulling from 37 sources with 1,283 benchmark records and 12,916 score observations. It ships a web dashboard, leaderboards, Pareto views, and a CLI for offline queries. **Why it matters:** Gives researchers a single source of truth to find and compare benchmarks instead of hunting across scattered papers/repos.
- **A Multimodal LLM-Driven Framework for Context-Aware UAV Emergency Landing Site Selection** — Hua et al. · https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.01163
  The framework pairs a lightweight segmentation pre-screen with a vision-language reasoning agent fusing remote-sensing imagery and POI data to flag semantic hazards (crowds, structures) invisible to geometric sensors. It significantly beats geometric baselines on the new ELSS benchmark and gives human-readable justifications. **Why it matters:** Shows VLM agents can add semantic risk-awareness to safety-critical robotics decisions beyond raw geometry.
- **Knowledge-as-Skill: A Structural Design for Autonomous Knowledge-Base Use by LLM Agents** — Jiangxu Wu · https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25991
  Knowledge-as-Skill reorganizes knowledge bases with a SKILL.md discovery layer, per-directory index.md navigation, and YAML-frontmatter documents so agents can decide what to retrieve rather than relying on blind RAG chunking. On the WixQA benchmark it beats reported Corpus2Skill numbers on Factuality (0.889) and Context Recall (0.816). **Why it matters:** Directly relevant to agent-skill design patterns — makes knowledge bases self-describing so agents retrieve smarter, not just more.
