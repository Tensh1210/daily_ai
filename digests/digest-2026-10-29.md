## 📄 Research Papers — Top Picks
- **From Code Generator to Engineering Companion: A Plant-aware Agent for PLC Programming** — Kersting, Benndorf · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28160
  Earlier RAG-based LLMs could generate vendor-compliant Structured Text for PLCs, but only as a one-shot pipeline with no knowledge of the specific plant. This paper presents a conversational copilot that ingests plant-specific context, supports chat-based explanation and debugging, and offers a graduated tool-access ladder that runs from chat to deployment. The engineer stays in control of every step that affects the real world. **Why it matters:** It is a concrete pattern for giving an agent tiered tool permissions in a safety-critical industrial domain.
- **The Shape of the DPO Hyperparameter Landscape: What to Measure, How It Moves with Scale, and When Not to Transfer It** — Mahadik, Salinas, Golebiowski, Klein · https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27895
  The authors run a dense hyperparameter grid for DPO (learning rate and β) across several model scales from one family. The optimal learning rate changes with model size, and hyperparameters do not transfer across training datasets. Validation loss is a poor proxy for choosing them, while intermediate checkpoints give a cheap early signal. **Why it matters:** Anyone fine-tuning with DPO should re-tune per dataset and model size instead of copying published configs.
