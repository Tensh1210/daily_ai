## 📄 Research Papers — Top Picks
- **WorldDraft: If Code Builds the World, Can Video Models Just Render It?** — Zhiyu Hou, Wen-Jie Shu et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.16217
  In this setup, multimodal coding agents build 4D worlds in code and video models only render them. WorldDraft is a benchmark of 432 paired cases across 48 executable 4D world families that tests whether renderers keep the prescribed kinematics and camera paths while applying text-specified appearance. Across 12 models, keeping the world intact and rendering the requested look pull against each other. More detailed drafts help structure but hurt appearance, and conflicting cues cause up to 64% source leakage. **Why it matters:** It shows where the "agent writes the scene, video model renders it" pipeline currently breaks.
- **PhysLoop: Harnessing LLMs' Physics Awareness for Self-Improving Video Generation Models** — Yuxuan Chen, Xiaojie Li et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.16748
  PhysLoop is a harness that guides and constrains LLMs as they generate, select, and correct videos. This distills their physics knowledge into physically consistent training data. It supports both controllable data construction and inference-time video repair. The release includes 100K videos and two eval sets, PhysLoop-Gen and PhysLoop-Repair. **Why it matters:** A practical route to self-improving video models with little human labeling, aimed at their weak spot in physics.
- **From Large Language Models to Efficient System One Models via Adaptive Computation** — Minh-Anh Nguyen · https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.16321
  When LLMs are turned into fast decision models, the decision information sits in the intermediate layers. The final six layers can be removed with no loss. The paper jointly trains a full model with prefix sub-models that share its final layers and decision head through self-distillation. A lightweight gate at each exit decides whether more computation is needed. On seven decision benchmarks it matches or beats independently trained models at every depth. **Why it matters:** One model can serve several latency budgets, which cuts inference cost for agent policies that make decisions.
- **UniSpark: Exploring Hybrid Attention for Multimodal Understanding and Generation** — Jiancheng Pan, Yuzheng Liu et al. · https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.16517
  UniSpark-9B is a unified Mixture-of-Transformer-Experts model. It combines full attention with GatedDeltaNet linear attention and handles VQA, text-to-image, editing, and interleaved generation. A blockwise protocol keeps noisy visual targets separate from later predictions. The model is trained with a four-stage curriculum on about 374M samples and generates images at up to 2K resolution. **Why it matters:** Evidence that hybrid linear/full attention can scale to unified understanding and generation models.
- **Factored One-sided Adam-Moment for Practical and Scalable SOAP** — Jaemyung Yu, Byeongho Heo, Sangdoo Yun, Dongyoon Han · https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.16905
  FOAM is a lightweight variant of the SOAP second-order optimizer. It preconditions only the input side, which removes one Kronecker factor and one eigendecomposition. It also replaces SOAP's dense rotated second moment with a factored row-by-column Adam-moment estimate. Under the K-FAC assumptions this estimate exactly recovers SOAP's moment, and the measured violation is only 15–20%. **Why it matters:** It brings second-order convergence speedups close to AdamW's memory and compute cost for transformer training.
